Building a garden has an order, and most of the cost of getting it wrong is
hidden. Drainage that was never solved shows up as a failed wall two winters
later. A patio built before the grading is settled has to come up again.

So the sequence runs the same way every time. Site preparation first: clearing,
grading, and making the water go where it should. Then hardscape, because
planting around a finished wall is easier than the reverse. Then the planting
itself, placed for the light and the soil it will actually live in. Irrigation
goes in laid out by hydrozone, so a lawn and a stand of natives are not sharing
a valve and one of them losing.

The company that drew the plan is the company that builds it, which removes the
gap where a design gets handed to somebody who was not in the room when it was
decided.

The full sequence, from the first visit through to the final walkthrough, is set
