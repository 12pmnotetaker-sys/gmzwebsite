Still young, and the side bed suits it: morning sun and shade through the worst
of the afternoon is what keeps the leaf edges from scorching.

Weekly water through the summer while the roots are still establishing. That
eases off as it settles in over the next few seasons.

We prune it lightly and only in winter, when the tree is dormant and the cuts
close cleanly.
