Yellow leaves with green veins, on the new growth rather than the old, is the
plant telling you it cannot take up enough iron. It is almost always the soil
rather than the plant: our ground runs alkaline enough that iron is present but
locked up where the roots cannot reach it.

Iron applied to the soil corrects it. The colour comes back over a few weeks,
and it holds for a season or two before it wants doing again.

This is routine care on a normal Wednesday, so there is nothing to price and
nothing to approve beyond telling us to go ahead.
