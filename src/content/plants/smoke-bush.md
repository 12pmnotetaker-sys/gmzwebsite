Nothing needed. It is doing what it should.

We cut it back hard in late winter, which is what keeps the leaf colour strong
and stops it going bare and leggy at the bottom. Left alone it flowers more and
colours less; cut back it colours more and barely flowers. Yours is pruned for
the colour.
