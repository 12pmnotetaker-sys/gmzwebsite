localNote: >-
  Woodside asks for the square footage of new landscaping and the square footage
  of rehabilitated landscaping as two separate numbers on the plan, using the
  state water ordinance's own definitions. Most towns ask for one figure, so
  this is the town where the distinction between a new garden and a rebuilt one
  has to be settled on paper before anything is submitted. Its tree
  requirements are equally exact. Trunks are measured four feet above grade,
  which is not the height its neighbours use, and the dripline of every
  significant tree has to be drawn wherever it falls over proposed work,
  including patios, pools, walls, fences, septic systems and utility runs
  rather than structures alone.
