  Palo Alto publishes something most towns do not: a landscape permit trigger
  worksheet that walks a homeowner through whether their project pulls in
  review at all, separately from the submittal guidelines themselves. It is the
  quickest way to settle the threshold question early, and it is the difference
  between designing with the water budget in mind and redrawing a planting plan
  after plan check.
