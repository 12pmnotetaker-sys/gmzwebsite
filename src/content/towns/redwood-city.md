localNote: >-
  Redwood City is where the yard is, and its tree rule is the one most often
  measured wrongly. A tree is protected once its trunk reaches thirty-eight
  inches around, roughly twelve inches across, and the measurement is taken
  anywhere between six and thirty-six inches above the ground rather than at
  the four and a half feet almost every neighbouring town uses. A low flare or
  a heavy base therefore brings a tree inside the ordinance here that would sit
  outside it a mile away. The permit is issued by the Parks and Recreation
  Director rather than by planning, so a tree question and a plan question go
  to different counters. A protected tree damaged without a permit has to be
  restored, or, where it cannot be, replaced within a year with a tree matched
  for size and species.
