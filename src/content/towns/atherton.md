localNote: >-
  Atherton replaced its own water-efficient landscaping chapter and adopted the
  state ordinance by reference, with revisions, in December 2019. Its building
  department publishes the water efficiency worksheet, the prescriptive option
  for smaller projects and the certificate of completion alongside its other
  submittal checklists, so the paperwork is knowable before design starts. The
  town also sets out its tree protection requirement in unusual detail,
  including the fencing specification, and expects that detail to appear on
  grading, demolition and building permit plans rather than being handled on
  site.
