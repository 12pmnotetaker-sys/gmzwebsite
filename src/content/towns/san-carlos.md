localNote: >-
  San Carlos is the town where the tree rules, rather than the water rules, are
  the thing to check first. Its protected tree ordinance was rewritten in 2022,
  and a permit is required to prune a protected tree, not only to remove one,
  which catches out work that would be routine maintenance anywhere else. A
  significant tree is any tree eleven inches in diameter measured at
  fifty-four inches above natural grade, redwoods are protected from fifteen
  inches at the same height, and the city arborist decides the application
  rather than a planner. Pruning permits carry a neighbour notice period of
  fourteen days, so a crew date has to be planned around the notice rather than
  the other way round.
