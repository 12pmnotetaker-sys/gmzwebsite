localNote: >-
  Belmont is the town where hardscape and irrigation, not only tree work, can
  need a tree permit. Removal is defined to include severely damaging a tree,
  and severe damage names trenching, excavating, grading and paving that
  intrudes on the root zone or canopy dripline, along with topping, excessive
  pruning and even overwatering, so a patio edge or a new valve run near a
  protected tree sits inside the ordinance. The protected set is wide. Coast
  live oak, valley oak, redwood, madrone, bay laurel and buckeye count from ten
  inches across measured four and a half feet up, and any species at all counts
  from fourteen. Multi-stemmed trees are measured by adding the three largest
  stems, which reaches the line at ten inches for those six natives and
  eighteen for everything else. The dripline used throughout is the canopy or
  the trunk diameter in inches multiplied by two feet, whichever is larger,
  which is usually wider than the shade on the ground suggests.
