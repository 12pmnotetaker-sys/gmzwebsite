We do. The drawings are done in house, and the company that draws the plan is
the company that builds it. That removes the gap where a design gets handed to
somebody who was not in the room when it was decided, and it means the person
who chose the levels and the planting is the one answering for them when the
crew is on site.

If you only want the drawings, that works too; see the answer on buying one
stage on its own.
