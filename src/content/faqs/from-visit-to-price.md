---
question: What happens between the first visit and a firm price?
short: From visit to price
phase: bridge
serviceLine: design-build
order: 10
published: true
---

The deciding, all of it on paper, where changing your mind costs nothing.

After the visit the site is read properly: dimensions, existing planting, soil,
drainage and the fall of the land, recorded rather than remembered. The brief is
written down so there is something to check the design against later. Then a
concept comes back, shown to you for a reaction rather than approval, and once
the direction is right the plan is resolved: specific planting, hardscape,
lighting, water and any outdoor living space.

The firm price arrives with the finished drawings, with costs against them and
a plain account of what building them will involve. Where the town requires a
permit, that is dealt with before anyone digs, and on the Peninsula that is more
often than people expect. The full sequence is on the [process page](/process).
