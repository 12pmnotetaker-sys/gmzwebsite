Draft, held until the permit policy is confirmed.

Whether you need one is the town's call, and on the Peninsula the answer is yes
more often than people expect: retaining walls above a certain height, work
near a protected tree, grading and drainage changes all tend to need review.
Permits and approvals are obtained where the town requires them, before anyone
digs, as a step in the design stage.

What this answer will add, once confirmed, is who applies, whether the town's
fees are inside the price or separate, and what a design-only client gets.
