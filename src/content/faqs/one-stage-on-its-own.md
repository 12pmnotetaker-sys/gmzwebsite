question: Can I just have the drawings, or just the maintenance?

phase: design

Yes. The work runs in three stages, design, construction and care, and most
clients take all three because a garden built by the people who drew it tends
to turn out as drawn. Any one of the three can be bought on its own.

Design on its own gets you the survey and levels, the concept, renders, the
planting plan and the hardscape and site layout, resolved. Maintenance on its
own starts with a walk of the property, and we look after gardens we had nothing
to do with as well as the ones we built.
