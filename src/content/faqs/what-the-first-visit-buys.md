question: What do I actually get for the consultation fee?

An hour on the property with the people who will design it, spent listening to
what you want the garden to do and looking at what is already there: the soil,
the drainage, the fall of the land, what is worth keeping.

It is a fixed, prepaid fee, agreed before the visit is booked. It is not a
deposit and it is not credited toward the drawings or the build, so what you are
paying for is the visit itself rather than a down payment on work you have not
agreed to. We say that here because the worst place to discover it is at
contract signing.

No drawing and no quote come out of the visit. What comes out of it is the brief
that everything after is checked against.
