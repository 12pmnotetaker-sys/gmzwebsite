Routine care on a schedule, done by people who know what the garden was
designed to be. Through the year that is pruning and seasonal planting, the
beds kept as planted rather than as they drift, establishment watering while
new planting takes, and the irrigation checked and adjusted as things fill in,
so the thirsty and the drought-tolerant are not on the same valve. Repairs and
replacements are part of it too.

Anything outside routine care is priced and approved by you before it happens,
so a visit never turns into a bill you did not expect.

It starts with a walk of the property, because pricing maintenance from a
photograph is guesswork. The walk is a fixed, prepaid price, and like the design
consultation it is not credited toward later work. We look after gardens we
built and gardens somebody else did.
