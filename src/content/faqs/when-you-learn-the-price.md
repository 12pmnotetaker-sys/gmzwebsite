Tell us what you need. You can contact us about a specific repair, planting
update, irrigation issue or ongoing garden maintenance, as well as a larger
landscape project. If you already have plans or a written scope, let us know.

We will review the work with you and explain what information or site visit
is needed before we can provide a price. A defined repair or maintenance
request does not automatically need a full garden design. We will confirm
whether the job is a good fit and explain any visit fee before booking.

For a new garden or a more involved renovation, design work may be needed to
establish the scope before construction can be priced.
