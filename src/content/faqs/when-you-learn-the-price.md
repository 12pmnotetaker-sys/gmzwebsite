A price comes from a plan, and at the start there is no plan yet. Any number
offered before the drawings is a guess rather than a quote, and it should not be
planned around.

The first visit produces no quote. What follows it is the site properly
measured, a brief written down, a concept to react to, and then a resolved
design. The price arrives with that: the finished drawings are presented with
costs against them and a plain account of what building them involves, so the
number you see is attached to something you can hold the build to.
