---
question: What happens when the crew leaves?
short: Handover and after
phase: after
serviceLine: design-build
order: 10
published: true
---

Three things, in order. The garden is walked with you and checked against the
drawings, and anything outstanding is written down rather than agreed on the
driveway. Then you are handed what the garden needs and when, because a new
planting is at its most fragile in its first year and the watering that suits
the lawn will drown the bed next to it. Then, if you want it, the same company
comes back to look after it: a garden is not finished when the crew leaves.

Maintenance is a separate agreement, priced from a walk of the property, and
there is no obligation to take it.
