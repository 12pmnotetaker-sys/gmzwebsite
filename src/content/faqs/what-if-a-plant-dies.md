Draft, held until a plant guarantee is decided.

A new planting is at its most fragile in its first year, and most losses come
down to water: too much, too little, or the wrong schedule for that bed. This
answer will say whether a plant that fails in that first period is replaced,
for how long, and whether that depends on who is doing the watering.
